# Multilingual Publishing

Translation alone is not localization. Search intent, content expectations, and
acceptable terminology vary across the United States, Japan, Korea, Taiwan,
Mainland China, and Spanish-speaking markets.

## Editorial checklist

1. Write the title and summary for the local search intent.
2. Use native terminology rather than transliterating every English keyword.
3. Test prompts in the target language.
4. Link to the most relevant localized destination.
5. State when a feature, price, or policy may vary by region.

The seven-language feeds provide source metadata, but local editors should rewrite
headlines and context for their audience rather than syndicating identical copy.
