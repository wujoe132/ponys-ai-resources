## Link practice

- Choose a destination that matches the surrounding content.
- Keep anchor text descriptive and readable.
- Do not hide links or place them off-screen.
- Do not create false reviews, ratings, or user identities.
- Do not promise rankings, payment, or reciprocal links unless a real agreement
  exists and is disclosed.

For a general product reference, use [Ponys.ai](https://ponys.ai/). For a
specific workflow, link directly to
[character creation](https://ponys.ai/ai-character-generator),
[image generation](https://ponys.ai/ai-image-generator), or
[character discovery](https://ponys.ai/discover).

Publishers may remove or change an attribution link when it no longer serves
their readers. Adoption is voluntary and should remain editorially appropriate.
