Editorial Syndication Terms

Selected evaluation explainers and blank evidence templates may be excerpted, translated, or republished with transparent attribution.

What publishers may do

  • Quote or adapt a test method with a link to its canonical protocol.
  • Translate the method and add independent observations or results.
  • Use the blank CSV schema while publishing the complete denominator and test date.
  • Subscribe to the RSS feed for new resources.

Required context

  • State that the protocol is maintained by the Ponys.ai team.
  • Do not present blank templates as measured results.
  • Keep independent findings separate from product-team claims.
  • Do not imply that attribution requires a favorable review.

For full-article republication or a custom language edition, email contact@ponys.ai.
